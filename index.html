Selected work

Sensor validation system

Precision Planting

A test stand, control software, data recording and transfer, and analysis pipeline for an optical seed-flow sensor. It produced the statistical evidence for whether the design met its ±2% requirement, and it showed that tighter tolerances and assembly changes cut sensor-to-sensor variance about 3x. I ran 50+ sensors on the bench and analyzed data from 250+ installed on farmer equipment over two seasons.

Heat map accuracy algorithm

Appareo Systems (Intelligent Ag)

A sensor with a working mechanical design but roughly ±60 to 80% accuracy. I removed a calibration step that skewed the reference value, corrected for product variation across materials, and built a heat map algorithm that brought accuracy to about ±5% in standard operating conditions.
